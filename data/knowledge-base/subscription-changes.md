# Subscription changes

## Upgrades

An upgrade takes effect immediately. The account receives credit for the unused portion of the previous plan and is charged a prorated amount for the new plan through the current billing date.

## Downgrades

A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal. Current plan features remain available until that date. The confirmation screen shows the future plan and effective date before the customer confirms.

## Cancellation

Cancellation stops the next renewal but does not erase the current paid period. Access continues through the displayed end date. Cancellation alone does not create or approve a refund.

# Duplicate charges

## Identify the charges

Two entries can represent a completed charge and a temporary authorization. Ask the customer to compare the amounts, dates, currencies, and the last four digits of the payment method. A temporary authorization normally disappears within seven calendar days.

## When both charges settled

If both entries are settled, acknowledge the duplicate and collect the invoice identifiers. Do not promise an immediate refund. Submit the case for refund review after confirming that the charges cover the same account, plan, and billing period.

## Plan upgrades

An upgrade can create a prorated charge while the previous plan closes. If two full-plan charges cover the same period, treat the case as a possible duplicate. If the periods differ or one amount is prorated, explain the dates and amounts before proposing review.

# Refund review policy

## Eligibility review

Refunds are not automatic. A customer may request review within 14 calendar days of the charge. The review considers service usage, duplicate billing, and applicable consumer protections. Support must not guarantee approval.

## Information to collect

Record the invoice identifier, charge date, amount, reason for the request, and whether the subscription is still active. Never ask for a full payment-card number or security code.

## Review outcome

The billing team communicates the decision after review. If approved, processing time depends on the payment provider and may take five to ten business days to appear on the original payment method.

# Account security and verification

## Suspected compromise

Treat unexpected sign-ins, changed credentials, or unknown activity as high priority. Tell the customer to reset their password from a trusted device and revoke unfamiliar sessions from Security settings. Escalate if they cannot sign in.

## Verification boundaries

Support may ask for the account email, recent non-sensitive activity, and an invoice identifier. Never request a password, one-time code, recovery code, full card number, or card security code.

## Multi-factor authentication

If a customer lost the authenticator device, direct them to a saved recovery code. Without a recovery code, route the case to identity verification; do not claim that multi-factor authentication has been disabled.

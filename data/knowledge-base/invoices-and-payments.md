# Invoices and payments

## Finding an invoice

Customers can find invoices in Billing under Payment history. An invoice is available after a payment reaches a completed state. Pending payments do not yet have a final invoice.

## Failed payments

Ask the customer to verify the card expiry date, billing address, and available funds. They may retry once after correcting the details. Repeated retries can trigger issuer controls, so the customer should contact the card issuer if the failure continues.

## Invoice corrections

Support can document a requested company name or address correction for review. Do not state that a historical invoice has been changed until the billing review is complete.

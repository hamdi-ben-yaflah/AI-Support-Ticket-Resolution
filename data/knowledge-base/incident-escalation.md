# Incident escalation

## Possible incident

Escalate when the same core function fails for multiple users, when the status page reports degradation, or when a complete outage blocks a customer. Record the first observed time, affected region, feature, and a concise symptom.

## Customer communication

Acknowledge the impact and say the issue is being investigated only after an incident has been confirmed. Do not provide an estimated recovery time unless it appears in an approved incident update.

## Security-related behavior

Unexpected data exposure, credential leakage, or cross-account access is a security concern, not ordinary troubleshooting. Tell the customer to stop sharing sensitive material and escalate immediately through the security path.

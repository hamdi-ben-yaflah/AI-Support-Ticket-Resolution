# Account access recovery

## Password reset

The customer can request a password-reset email from the sign-in page. The link expires after 30 minutes and works once. Ask the customer to check spam filtering and confirm they are using the email address associated with the account.

## No access to the email address

Do not change the sign-in email based only on a ticket. Route the case to identity verification. Support must not request a password, recovery code, or full payment-card number.

## Locked account

Multiple failed attempts can temporarily lock sign-in for 15 minutes. The customer should wait for the lock to expire before trying a fresh reset link.
